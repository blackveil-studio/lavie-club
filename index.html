The Club

A space to grow, not just to escape.

La Vie Club is a community first — entrepreneurs, experts and women building something of their own, across Kyiv, Warsaw and the rest of Europe. Women who take their ambition seriously, and refuse to let it be the only thing they take seriously.

We show up for each other on a stretch mat in a park, across a tennis net, over a long brunch, and on a beach three countries away. Different room, same intention — presence, honesty, and each other.
