The Club

Not an escape
from your life.
A return to it.

La Vie Club is a private circle for entrepreneurs, experts and women building something of their own — across Kyiv, Warsaw and the rest of Europe. Women who take their careers seriously, and refuse to let that be the only thing they take seriously.

We meet on yachts off Mykonos and on a stretch mat in a Warsaw park. Over brunch, over a match, over a glass of something good. The setting changes; what we come back for doesn't — quality, in company and in life.

"You don't need another trip. You need people who make you want to come back."
